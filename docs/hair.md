# HairCam

Among the many ways people enhance their appearance, haircuts are less reversible than cosmetics or clothing.
Our app helps users visualize hairstyles before getting a haircut or buying hairpieces or wigs.
These visualizations and recommendations are based on and tailored to customers' characteristics and preferences, including face shape, age, hair texture and type (thick or thin, straight or curly, color, etc.), local climate (such as humidity, which can greatly affect how hair looks), and lifestyle (maintenance needs and suitability for sports or work, for example).
In addition to visualizing and recommending hairstyles, the app connects users with service providers, such as hairstylists who can create a specific haircut, and provides information about costs, available appointments, and products such as suitable shampoo, conditioner, and hair dryers.
